# Privacy Policy **Service:** CN Fun Cartoons Automation **Effective date:** 20 November 2026 ## 1. Overview CN Fun Cartoons Automation is a personal project for preparing and publishing videos to social-media accounts that its owner chooses to connect. This policy describes the intended data handling of the project. ## 2. Information processed Depending on the integrations enabled, the project may process: - Video files, captions, titles, and other publishing information supplied for a post. - Basic account/profile information returned by a platform after the account owner authorizes access. - Platform authorization tokens required to perform approved actions. The project does not ask users to send passwords or one-time verification codes to the project owner. ## 3. How information is used Information is used only to create, upload, or publish the content requested by the account owner, and to diagnose automation errors. The project does not sell personal information or use it for advertising. ## 4. Storage and security Where configured, API credentials are stored as repository or automation secrets and should not be placed in public source files. Video files and workflow logs may be processed or retained by the hosting and platform services according to their own retention settings and policies. No claim is made that data is stored only in one country. ## 5. Sharing Information is sent to the social-media platform selected by the account owner and to the hosting/automation provider needed to run the project. Those providers process information under their own privacy policies. The project does not sell information to data brokers. ## 6. Retention and deletion Content and logs may remain in the repository's automation history or in the connected platform until deleted under that provider's controls. The account owner can revoke platform authorization and remove stored secrets from the repository settings. Deleting a post from this project does not necessarily delete copies held by a platform. ## 7. Children's privacy This project may publish cartoon content intended for a general or child audience, but it is not designed to collect personal information directly from children. Do not submit children's personal information through the project. ## 8. Your choices You may stop using the project, revoke connected-app permissions through the platform, and remove credentials from repository secrets. Requests about data handled by TikTok or another platform should also be directed to that platform. ## 9. Changes and contact This policy may be updated when project features or integrations change. For questions, contact the project owner through the GitHub repository: https://github.com/Murtuzahussain110/nature-shorts-automation.
