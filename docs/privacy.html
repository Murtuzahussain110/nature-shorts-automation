Privacy Policy

Service: CN Fun Cartoons Automation
Effective date: 22 November 2026

1. Overview

CN Fun Cartoons Automation is a project used by its owner to prepare original cartoon videos and publish them to social-media accounts that the owner chooses to connect. This policy describes the information processed by the project.

2. Information processed

  • Video files, captions, titles, and publishing settings supplied for a post.
  • Basic account/profile information returned by TikTok after the account owner authorizes access.
  • OAuth authorization tokens required to perform the actions the account owner requests.
  • Technical logs and status information needed to diagnose failed uploads.

The project does not request TikTok passwords or one-time verification codes.

3. How information is used

Information is used to connect the account, prepare and upload videos selected by the account owner, check publishing status, and troubleshoot errors. Personal information is not sold and is not used by this project for advertising.

4. Storage and security

OAuth tokens are stored in the project's Cloudflare Worker KV storage and are used by the Worker to refresh access tokens and call TikTok APIs. Automation credentials are stored as GitHub Actions secrets. Video files and workflow logs may be processed or retained by GitHub, Cloudflare, and TikTok according to their own settings and policies. No claim is made that information is stored only in one country.

5. Sharing

Information is sent to TikTok when the account owner connects the account or submits a video for publishing. Technical data is also processed by GitHub and Cloudflare to host and run the project. These providers process information under their own privacy policies. This project does not sell information to data brokers.

6. Retention and deletion

OAuth tokens remain in Cloudflare KV until the account owner disconnects or deletes them. The account owner can revoke this project's access through TikTok's connected-app settings and can request removal of project-held tokens through the project owner. Workflow artifacts and logs are retained according to the repository's configured retention period. Deleting data here does not necessarily delete copies already held by TikTok or another provider.

7. Children's privacy

The project may publish cartoon videos suitable for a general or child audience, but it is not designed to collect personal information directly from children. Do not submit children's personal information through the project.

8. Your choices

You may stop using the project and revoke its access in TikTok's security or connected-app settings. You may also contact the project owner to request deletion of project-held authorization data.

9. Changes and contact

This policy may be updated when the project's features or integrations change. For questions, contact the project owner through the GitHub repository.
